About MBD

Made by people who'd rather build than bill.

MBD started in 2021 with a stubborn idea: most freelance platforms reward whoever bids fastest, not whoever ships best. So we flipped it. Every person you work with at MBD has been peer-reviewed by other specialists in their craft — designers vet designers, engineers vet engineers, researchers vet researchers.

That's why we don't sell time. We sell outcomes. A landing page that converts. A model that learns. An app that doesn't crash on launch day. Track every milestone live in your dashboard, talk to your team in real time, pay only when each phase ships.
